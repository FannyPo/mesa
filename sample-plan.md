## Sample 7-Day Family Meal Plan

**Sample plan, shared with permission. Household details are illustrative.**

Household of 4 · $275 weekly · Blood-sugar safe for Adult 1 · Fuel for two teen athletes

**How this plan feeds everyone.** The dinners are built blood-sugar-smart for the adult managing blood sugar — but the teen athletes need more fuel, not less. Look for the TEEN ADD notes: that's where the active kids get extra rice, fruit, or a bigger portion. Adult 1 keeps the smaller, lower-carb version of the same meal. One dinner, two portion sizes. Nobody cooks twice.

---

## How This Plan Works

1. **One meal, scaled per person.** Everyone eats the same dinner. Adult 1 gets the blood-sugar portion (smaller carbs, more vegetables); the teens get bigger servings and extra carbs to match how much they burn at practice.

2. **The blood-sugar rule, for Adult 1.** White rice, white bread, and instant oats spike blood sugar fast. Adult 1's plate swaps in brown rice, oats, and vegetables. The teens can have the regular versions — they're working it off.

3. **Batch on Sunday, fast all week.** One hour Sunday means weeknight dinners are 15–20 minutes.

---

## Sunday Prep — 75 Minutes

- Bake 12 chicken thighs (two pans, 400°F, 40 min). Keep some whole; shred the rest → lunches and two dinners.
- Cook a big pot of brown rice (2 lbs dry) AND a pot of white rice for the teens, or just make extra brown and let teens add fruit/bread for carbs.
- Hard-boil a dozen eggs → fast breakfasts and post-practice protein snacks.
- Cut veggie sticks (carrots, peppers) → grab-and-go snacks for the teens.

## MONDAY

**Breakfast — Oatmeal + eggs (8 min)**
Rolled oats with cinnamon for everyone; add 2 hard-boiled eggs each for the teens. TEEN ADD banana sliced on the oats.

**Lunch — Chicken & rice bowls**
Shredded chicken + brown rice + vegetables + salsa. TEEN ADD bigger scoop of rice + cheese.

**Dinner — Baked chicken + roasted broccoli + brown rice (20 min)**
Adult 1: ½ cup brown rice, extra broccoli. TEEN ADD 1+ cup rice, dinner roll, second piece of chicken.

---

## TUESDAY

**Breakfast — Greek yogurt + fruit + granola (3 min)**
Plain Greek yogurt + berries. TEEN ADD granola and honey for the carbs.

**Lunch — Turkey & cheese wraps + veggie sticks**

**Dinner — Turkey tacos (15 min)**
Ground turkey + black beans + cabbage + salsa. Adult 1: 2 tacos, whole-wheat, loaded with veg. TEEN ADD 3–4 tacos + rice on the side.

> Protein + beans + cabbage slow blood sugar for Adult 1. Whole-wheat tortillas beat a big side of white rice here — but the teens can pile it on.

---

## WEDNESDAY

**Breakfast — Scrambled eggs + spinach + toast (10 min)**
Adult 1 skips or has 1 slice whole-grain toast; teens get 2.

**Lunch — Chicken & spinach bowls**

**Dinner — Sheet pan chicken, sweet potato & broccoli (30 min, oven)**
Adult 1: half sweet potato, extra broccoli. TEEN ADD whole sweet potato + extra chicken.

---

## THURSDAY

**Breakfast — Oatmeal + eggs (8 min)**

**Lunch — Leftover sheet pan over rice**

**Dinner — Beef & vegetable stir-fry (15 min)**
Lean beef + mixed vegetables + soy sauce + ginger. Adult 1: cauliflower rice or ½ cup brown rice. TEEN ADD full bowl of rice or noodles.

> Cauliflower rice has ~5g carbs per cup vs. 45g for white rice. Adult 1 gets the swap; the teens get the real thing to refuel.

---

## FRIDAY

**Lunch — Hard-boiled eggs + veggie sticks + fruit**

**Dinner — Baked fish + quinoa + green salad (20 min)**
White fish or salmon, lemon + garlic. Adult 1: quinoa + big salad. TEEN ADD extra quinoa + bread.

---

## SATURDAY

**Breakfast — Veggie omelettes (15 min)**

**Lunch — Fridge clearout (no cooking)**

**Dinner — Family taco/rice bowl night**
Use up remaining proteins and rice. Everyone builds their own bowl — Adult 1 low-carb, teens loaded.

---

## SUNDAY

**Breakfast — Big family eggs + fruit**

Then shop and start again.

---

## For the Athletes — A Note

Teen athletes need carbs around practice — fruit, rice, oats, whole-grain bread before and after training help recovery. Don't put growing, active teens on Adult 1's lower-carb portions. The blood-sugar swaps are for Adult 1; the kids just need more of the real thing, timed around their sport.
